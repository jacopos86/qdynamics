## Variational dynamics foundations

- McLachlan 1964 — least-squares projected dynamics.
- Dirac/Frenkel — only if explicitly comparing variational principles.
- Li--Benjamin 2017 — early variational quantum simulator.
- Yuan et al. 2019 — variational quantum simulation theory.
- Endo et al. 2020 — variational simulation of general processes.
- Benedetti et al. 2021 — hardware-efficient time evolution.

## Direct adaptive/projection comparators

- Barison--Vicentini--Carleo 2021, pVQD — fixed projected variational dynamics.
- Yao et al. 2021, AVQDS — adaptive variational dynamics using McLachlan-distance information.
- Linteau et al. 2024, adaptive pVQD — circuit growth during projected dynamics.
- Zhang et al. 2025, compressed AVQDS / AVQDS(T) — compressed/tiled adaptive dynamics and reduced measurements.
- Miessen--Ollitrault--Tavernelli 2021, spin-boson McLachlan dynamics — required cautionary mixed-system benchmark and noise comparison.

## Digital simulation baselines

- Suzuki product formulas.
- Childs--Su lattice/product-formula simulation.
- Childs--Ostrander--Su randomized product formulas.
- Campbell qDRIFT.

## Mixed fermion--boson / encoding context

- Macridin et al. electron--phonon simulation.
- Sawaya et al. d-level/bosonic encodings.
- Li et al. variational basis state encoder.
- Denner et al. hybrid electron--phonon method.

## Citation discipline

- Do not claim adaptive McLachlan dynamics is new.
- Cite AVQDS/adaptive pVQD before describing online growth.
- Cite our figures/tables/protocols for append/prune performance.
- Cite `MATH/Math.md`/method equations for stay/append/prune/repair definitions.
