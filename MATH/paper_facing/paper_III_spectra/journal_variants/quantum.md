# Paper III Quantum Variant

Created: 2026-05-09  
Role: venue-specific planning notes, not a manuscript copy.

## Fit thesis

Quantum is the best Paper III target if the manuscript is written as a transparent technical method paper: assumptions early, algorithmic object precise, benchmarks reproducible, limitations explicit.

## Title direction

Preferred:

> Geometry-Selected QSE Manifolds for Mixed Fermion--Boson Spectra

Backup:

> Selected Excitation Manifolds for Mixed Fermion--Boson Spectra

## Abstract shape

1. State problem: mixed fermion--boson spectra require compact excitation manifolds, not only ground-state preparation.
2. State limitation: fixed QSE alphabets can overbuy measurements or miss mixed/polaronic directions.
3. State method: excitation records selected by probe-transition gain, overlap-metric novelty, residual-Schur spectral gain, conditioning, and cost.
4. State evidence: spectral-window, transition-strength, conditioning, and measurement-proxy benchmarks against fixed-alphabet QSE and excited-state baselines.
5. State limitation honestly if finite-shot/noise evidence is partial.

## First-two-pages requirement for agents

By the end of page 2, a Quantum-style draft should identify:

- the prepared seed state assumption;
- the excitation record definition;
- the nonorthogonal QSE metric/eigenproblem;
- the record-selection rule;
- the main comparator set;
- the main evidence type.

## Main-text structure

1. Introduction and contributions.
2. Excitation records and QSE metric.
3. Geometry/cost acquisition rule.
4. Spectral observables and regularization.
5. Frozen vs live excited-state propagation.
6. Benchmarks and ablations.
7. Limitations/reproducibility.

## Evidence bar

Minimum:

- fixed-alphabet QSE baselines;
- qEOM/q-sc-EOM/VQD/SSVQE comparison or a scoped explanation;
- ablations isolating metric novelty, residual-Schur gain, conditioning guard, cost penalty, and root tracking;
- reproducible alphabets, root windows, regularization thresholds, and measurement proxy definitions.

Do not target Quantum with a placeholder abstract or without explicit assumptions.
