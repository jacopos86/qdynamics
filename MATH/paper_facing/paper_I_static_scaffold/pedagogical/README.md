# Paper-I Pedagogical Method Sequence

This folder collects the source/PDF pairs for the Paper-I method notes in their
intended reading order. The first three PDFs are preserved copies of the
already-printed documents and remain byte-identical at their original paths.

1. `paper_i_metric_schur_prune_response_pedagogical_20260707.pdf`
   introduces projective tangents, metric projection, Schur response, and
   rollback-safe pruning.
2. `paper_i_joint_metric_schur_batch_selection_pedagogical_20260711.pdf`
   builds joint singleton and batch admission on that notation.
3. `paper_i_formal_manifold_propagation_pedagogical_20260712.pdf`
   begins after admission and develops supported tangent frames, transport,
   curvature recycling, and transactional reoptimization.
4. `paper_i_joint_reduction_and_formal_manifold_method_synthesis_20260713.pdf`
   is a journal-facing continuation volume. Three separately titled chapters
   present Paper-I Singleton-Response SNAKE, Joint-Response Batch SNAKE, and
   Formal-Manifold SNAKE as peer routes. The chapters reuse the first three
   documents for foundational derivations and retain separate selection laws,
   terminal decision objects, optimizer state, and transaction state.
5. `paper_i_formal_manifold_outer_information_transport_pedagogical_20260716.pdf`
   develops outer-iteration information persistence for Formal-Manifold SNAKE.
   It derives exact same-ray frame extension, supported projected-logical
   whitening, split metric-and-frame prediction, deterministic geometric
   closure, event-driven Procrustes anchoring, outer secants, and curvature
   recycling with a unique-primitive information ledger.

The continuation is a Paper-I-facing support artifact. The manuscript source
`MATH/paper_details/Paper_I.tex` remains unchanged. The continuation states the
raw support test, ridge, whitening bridge, trust-boundary reading, JR full
Gram/Schur model, and FM transport and checkpoint contracts explicitly.
