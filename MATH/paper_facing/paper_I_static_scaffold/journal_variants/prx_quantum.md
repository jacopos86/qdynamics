## Prospective editorial criterion

Primary route: **exceptional capability**.

Present SNAKE as a transferable capability for acquiring expressive adaptive
variational manifolds under estimator and compiled-circuit constraints. The
manuscript must establish this through one coherent mathematical principle,
fair modern baselines, causal ablations, cross-family evidence, realized
resource validation, and reproducible settings. Do not substitute stronger
adjectives for missing evidence.

## Editorial posture

- Preserve the technically dense authorial voice while simplifying notation,
  definition order, and method flow.
- Distinguish the central candidate-position acquisition principle from
  optional search policies such as batching, beam continuation, Pauli-child
  exploration, insertion, and ablation.
- Keep selection-time resource models separate from estimator work, shots, and
  final compiled-circuit metrics.
- State unfavorable regimes and unmatched comparison conditions directly.
- Keep missing fidelities and provisional canonical settings visible as
  unresolved evidence rather than fabricating or deleting them.
- Separate the algorithm definition from optimizer protocols. Current main
  results use Powell; SPSA material remains provisional support for possible
  appendix studies unless source evidence says otherwise.
