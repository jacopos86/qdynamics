## Official scope

Physical Review B covers significant developments in condensed matter and materials physics. It is strongest for papers where model-Hamiltonian physics, correlated materials, phases, spectra, or electron--phonon phenomena are central.

## Article types and formatting expectations

Regular Articles have no length limit. Letters have a 4,500-word limit; Comments/Replies are 3,500 words; Errata are supported. PRB requires titles in all published-paper references and uses APS style/data policies.

## Abstract style

Abstract should state the model/physical problem and quantitative finding, not only the algorithmic method.

## Introduction style

Introduction should establish condensed-matter/electron--phonon relevance before quantum-algorithm details if PRB is the target.

## Related-work density

Moderate. Cite physics/model literature and computational methods; QIS literature is necessary but should not overwhelm the physics framing.

## Methods/results balance

Results/physics interpretation should be stronger than in a pure algorithm paper. Methods can be detailed, but physical observables should carry the narrative.

## Equation tolerance

High for condensed-matter models and derivations when tied to physical observables.

## Figure/table conventions

Figures should emphasize phase/observable/trajectory/resource behavior. Tables should include model parameters and reproducibility metadata where appropriate.

## Data/code availability expectations

APS data availability statement required; public data/code encouraged and nonpublic data must be justified and shareable on reasonable request.

## Cover-letter or submission guidance

APS cover-letter guidance applies. PRB joint submissions are possible; Letters should justify priority and format.

## Why Paper I fits / does not fit

Paper I fits only conditionally if the scaffold construction is tied to Hubbard--Holstein/electron--phonon physics and benchmark observables. It is a weak fit for a generic ADAPT algorithm paper.

## Why Paper II fits / does not fit

Paper II fits conditionally if driven mixed fermion--boson dynamics, observables, and model-Hamiltonian conclusions dominate. It is weak if it reads solely as a VQA controller paper.

## Evidence threshold that would make this journal realistic

Model-physics motivation, comparisons to classical model methods where appropriate, clear observable outcomes, and resource claims tied to physical benchmark protocols.

## Claims too strong for this journal without more evidence

Generic QIS novelty claims, quantum advantage rhetoric, or hardware-readiness claims detached from physical evidence.

## Reusable drafting guidance

- Lead with Hubbard--Holstein/electron--phonon physics if choosing PRB.
- Make observables and parameter regimes central.
- Use algorithm details to support physics, not replace it.
- Include reference titles in BibTeX/final references.
