\section*{Parameter manifest}
\small
All energies use same-cutoff exact diagonalization. Reference, fidelity,
plotting, and compilation are reporting-only. Canonical SNAKE and Append rows
use logical-estimator accounting
\texttt{paper\_\allowbreak{}i\_\allowbreak{}clean\_\allowbreak{}algorithm\_\allowbreak{}s\_\allowbreak{}alg\_\allowbreak{}v3} under
\texttt{required\_\allowbreak{}executed\_\allowbreak{}logical\_\allowbreak{}scalar\_\allowbreak{}estimator\_\allowbreak{}invocations\_\allowbreak{}v1}. Any historical Geo-ADAPT
support accounting is labelled noncanonical in its method contract.
Bosons use \texttt{binary} encoding; Pauli words use
\texttt{blocked} ordering with
\texttt{open} boundaries. Drive enabled:
\texttt{false}. Same-cutoff reference:
\texttt{true}.
