\[

## Dimer Liouvillian convention

After the inactive center oscillator is factored out, the implemented
Hamiltonian is

\[
H(t)=
-t_{\rm hop}(X_\uparrow+X_\downarrow)
+\omega a^\dagger a
+h_z(t)(Z_\uparrow+Z_\downarrow),
\qquad
h_z(t)=\frac{V(t)}{2}+\kappa(a+a^\dagger),
\]

with \(\kappa=g/\sqrt2\).  The Heisenberg Liouvillian is
\(\mathcal L O=i[H,O]\).  Its elementary actions are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L a&=-i\omega a-i\kappa(Z_\uparrow+Z_\downarrow),\\
\mathcal L a^\dagger&=i\omega a^\dagger+i\kappa(Z_\uparrow+Z_\downarrow),\\
\mathcal L X_s&=-2h_zY_s,\\
\mathcal L Y_s&=2t_{\rm hop}Z_s+2h_zX_s,\\
\mathcal L Z_s&=-2t_{\rm hop}Y_s,
\end{aligned}
\qquad s\in\{\uparrow,\downarrow\}.
\]

The product rule for commutators then fixes the first mixed layer.  For one
spin \(s\), with \(\bar s\) the opposite spin and \(n=a^\dagger a\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L(aX_s)
={}&-i\omega aX_s-VaY_s
-2\kappa(a^2+n)Y_s-\kappa Y_s
-i\kappa Z_{\bar s}X_s,\\
\mathcal L(aY_s)
={}&-i\omega aY_s+2t_{\rm hop}aZ_s+VaX_s
+2\kappa(a^2+n)X_s+\kappa X_s
-i\kappa Z_{\bar s}Y_s,\\
\mathcal L(aZ_s)
={}&-i\omega aZ_s-2t_{\rm hop}aY_s
-i\kappa I-i\kappa Z_{\bar s}Z_s,
\end{aligned}
\]

and Hermitian conjugation gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L(a^\dagger X_s)
={}&i\omega a^\dagger X_s-Va^\dagger Y_s
-2\kappa(a^{\dagger2}+n)Y_s-\kappa Y_s
+i\kappa Z_{\bar s}X_s,\\
\mathcal L(a^\dagger Y_s)
={}&i\omega a^\dagger Y_s+2t_{\rm hop}a^\dagger Z_s
+Va^\dagger X_s+2\kappa(a^{\dagger2}+n)X_s+\kappa X_s
+i\kappa Z_{\bar s}Y_s,\\
\mathcal L(a^\dagger Z_s)
={}&i\omega a^\dagger Z_s-2t_{\rm hop}a^\dagger Y_s
+i\kappa I+i\kappa Z_{\bar s}Z_s.
\end{aligned}
\]

These equations use the infinite-boson commutator.  In a finite Fock space of
dimension \(d\), \([a,a^\dagger]=I-d|d-1\rangle\langle d-1|\); the associated
top-level terms are cutoff diagnostics and must be retained in a matrix audit.

The same-spin products have already reduced through Pauli multiplication.
After centering and removing the archive and mixed spans, the first genuine
unresolved layer is therefore generated by

\[
\left\{
a^2X_s,a^2Y_s,
nX_s,nY_s,
a^{\dagger2}X_s,a^{\dagger2}Y_s
\right\}
\]

and

\[
\left\{
Z_{\bar s}X_s,
Z_{\bar s}Y_s,
Z_{\bar s}Z_s
\right\}.
\]

The first set is the operator origin of the connected electron--two-phonon
term; the second is the opposite-spin covariance omitted by the archive
correlation equation.  This reproduces the two non-Pauli terms isolated by the
independent exact derivative audit.  Centering adds lower-order connection
terms but does not change these new operator types.

## State-adapted resolved and unresolved spaces

At state \(X\), let the archive tangent vectors be
\(|U_a(X)\rangle=Y_a(X)|\Psi\rangle\), and realify them with their phase
partners.  Their Gram is the joint archive Gram \(\mathfrak G_X\).  The
state-adapted orthogonal projector is

\[
\mathcal P_X|r\rangle
=\mathbb U_X\mathfrak G_X^+\mathbb U_X^{\mathsf T}|r\rangle,
\qquad
\mathcal Q_X=I-\mathcal P_X,
\]

on the supported real tangent range.  The implemented residualization of the
mixed frame is exactly \(\mathcal Q_X\mathbb W_X\).

Because \(\mathcal P_X\) changes with the state, eliminating the unresolved
component is not governed by \(\mathcal Q_X\mathcal L\) alone.  If
\(q=\mathcal Q_Xz\), then

\[
\dot q
=
(\mathcal Q_X\mathcal L-\dot{\mathcal P}_X)q
+(\mathcal Q_X\mathcal L-\dot{\mathcal P}_X)\mathcal P_Xz.
\]

The exact reduced equation therefore contains a preparation-dependent initial
slip and a time-ordered memory kernel generated by the state-adapted
orthogonal dynamics.  Dropping \(\dot{\mathcal P}_X\) would silently replace
the moving Gram geometry by a fixed projection.
