## Negative results first

1. **The 31-coordinate joint-Gram energy-neutral barrier has no long horizon at
   the pinned protocol.** Under a 150-minute wall budget toward `t = 20` the
   default `slsqp` lane stopped with its last stored sample at `t = 0.30`
   (solver time `t = 0.3407` at the budget stop), after `9000.2 s`, 46,034
   accepted DOP853 steps, 650,243 right-hand-side evaluations and 26,324,792
   half-space projections; the named `active_set` backend under the same budget
   stopped at `t = 0.35` (solver time `t = 0.3536`), `9000.1 s`, 48,878 steps,
   688,322 evaluations and 28,457,232 projections. Neither the post-pulse
   window `t >= 4` nor the spectral window `10 <= t` is reached by either lane,
   so the maximum post-pulse drift from `E(4)` and the polarization spectrum
   are not measurable here and are reported as unavailable with the horizon
   reached given instead.

2. **The cost is a step-size collapse, not a slow right-hand side.** The first
   554 accepted steps carry the trajectory to `t = 0.1898` at a mean accepted
   step of `3.4e-4`; the remaining 45,480 steps carry it `0.151` further at a
   mean accepted step of `3.3e-6`, a hundredfold collapse. Horizon gained per
   wall minute, read off the run's own progress trace: `0.01440` over
   `60`--`120 s`, `0.00499` over `300`--`360 s`, `0.00120` over
   `1201`--`1261 s`, `0.00070` over `3004`--`3064 s`, and `0.00060` over the
   last minute at `8897`--`8957 s`. The half-space projections per accepted
   step do not rise with it (next section), so the loss is in the accepted step
   size.

3. **Over the horizon it does reach, the barrier is about a tenth more accurate
   than the velocity it corrects, and the Eq. (112) archive EOM is two orders
   of magnitude more accurate than either.** The joint-Gram lane runs
   `closed_electron_phonon_cone_projected_rhs` with

## The three RMS conventions, and which line uses which

The lines this run is placed next to do not share one convention, so every
number above names its own.

| name | definition | producer | lines that use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coordinate_scalar_rms` | `sqrt(mean over samples and over the 31 unscaled closed scalar coordinates of (lane - exact)^2)` | `adaptive_positive_moment_analysis._score`, key `all_coordinate_scalar_rms_error` | the record's both-cones `0.128607` and uncorrected `0.193205` over `t = 0` to 20 (worklog:2781-2792) |
| `coordinate_l2_time_rms` | trapezoidal time-RMS of the unscaled 31-coordinate Euclidean distance | `score_coupling_controller_exact_reference._time_rms` | round 2's ten-coordinate barrier rows |
| `all31_scaled_rms` | `sqrt(mean of ((lane - exact) / development_coordinate_scales)^2)` | `analyze_adaptive_packet_event_ablation` | the adaptive-packet route's `t = 0` to 98 line `0.063649` |

The scale vector of `all31_scaled_rms` is recomputed by
`multi_coherent_scores.development_coordinate_scales` from the exact cutoff-16
path of the run that produces it, so two runs whose exact lanes cover different
horizons do not share it: round 2's scales come from `t = 0` to 100 and this
round's from `t = 0` to 20, which is why the same Eq. (112) trajectory over
`t = 0` to 20 scores `0.602005` there and `0.748812` here. The two unscaled
conventions are unaffected and agree between the runs to every printed digit
(`1.025151`, `0.184065`).

Definitional finding, established not asserted: the record's comparison line
"0.193205 uncorrected archive EOM" over `t = 0` to 20 is the **plain** archive
EOM, `closed_scalar_rhs` with no Eq. (112) initial-residual subtraction, in the
`coordinate_scalar_rms` convention. Round 2's stored plain lane scores
`0.193252` on the 401 samples of the 0.05 grid over `t = 0` to 20 against the
exact cutoff-16 reference, while its Eq. (112) lane
(`closed_residual_subtracted_rhs`) scores `0.184065` on the same samples. The
`4.7e-5` absolute gap to the record's printed `0.193205` is the difference
between this DOP853 protocol and the record's SSPRK(3,3) at `h = 0.0025`. This
extends round 2's finding, which established the same identification for the
polarization lane. The plain lane propagated in this repair round reproduces
round 2's stored plain lane on all 401 shared samples to a maximum absolute
coordinate difference of `1.1366e-14`, at identical sample times.

## Reading

The 31-coordinate joint-Gram energy-neutral barrier is not a long-horizon
controller at this protocol and cannot be put in a column next to the record's
both-cones `t = 0` to 20 line: 150 minutes of the default backend buys
`t = 0.30` and of the named backend `t = 0.35`, against the fixed-dictionary
APCM both-cones run's `t = 20` in `5,832.34 s` (worklog:2781-2792) and the
round-2 ten-coordinate barrier's `t = 79.95` in `1,500 s`. Extrapolating run
1's final measured rate, `0.00060` of horizon per wall minute, `t = 20` would
take about 23 days of the same wall clock, and that rate was still falling.

On the horizon it does reach, the barrier does what a containment controller is
supposed to do and pays for it in wall time, not in accuracy. The velocity it
corrects, the plain archive EOM, leaves the joint `(rho, N, A, C)` cone before
`t = 0.30` (minimum joint moment eigenvalue `-2.284502e-2`) and calls for a
correction of norm up to `0.3954804`; the corrected trajectory stays inside the
cone (`2.067511e-5`) and is about a tenth closer to exact than the trajectory
it repairs in both unscaled conventions (`0.897645` and `0.892126` of it), and
a third closer in the scaled one (`0.642204`). What it costs is the step size:
the corrected velocity is stiff where the uncorrected one is not, and the
obstruction is step rejection rather than the price of a correction -- rounds 1
and 2 took 24 percent and 35 percent off the right-hand side, this round's
named minimum-norm-point backend takes another 5.5 percent off the wall time
per right-hand-side evaluation (`0.01384 s` to `0.01308 s`), and all of that
together buys `0.05` of horizon.

The lane that needs no repair on this window is the Eq. (112) archive EOM,
which is a different velocity: it stays inside the joint cone
(`2.892080e-5`), calls for corrections of norm at most `7.9709e-6`, is 98.4
times closer to exact than the barrier in `coordinate_l2_time_rms`, and reaches
`t = 20` in `0.8 s`. Over `t = 0` to 20 that same lane is outside the bosonic
cone at `-0.30503297` and scores `1.02515085`, so its accuracy on the first
`0.30` is not a statement about the horizon this route is aimed at.

The pinned `activation_margin` of `1e-5` is within a factor of three of the
joint Gram minimum eigenvalue the exact trajectory itself carries over this
window (`2.893142e-5`), so the barrier is on at every sampled time of its own
lane from `t = 0`. Whether that setting is what makes the corrected velocity
stiff is measurable and was not measured; changing it changes the declared
controller, which is a user decision.

   record's both-cones `0.128607` would need, is out of reach on this machine

   `h = 0.0025`, not by an adaptive integrator; a fixed-step joint-Gram lane
   would not be stopped by step rejection, but it is a different scheme from
   the pinned protocol this task fixes. Whether that is the right comparison is
   a scope question for the parent.
3. `subtract_initial_residual=False` is round 1's pinned joint-Gram setting and
   is why this lane corrects the plain archive EOM while the default
   ten-coordinate lane corrects Eq. (112). The two barriers are therefore not
   measured against the same uncorrected velocity, and that is a property of
