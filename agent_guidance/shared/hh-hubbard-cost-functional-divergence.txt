COST-FUNCTIONAL DIVERGENCE: Hubbard--Holstein vs pure-Hubbard RA runs
Written 2026-09-06. TENTATIVE -- the reruns are the fix; this note is the
specification for what the rerun must change. Do not edit results to match it.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
The completed Paper-I Hubbard--Holstein (L=2, six regimes) and pure-Hubbard
(L=3, L=4) campaigns were ranked by DIFFERENT cost functionals. Neither the
manuscript nor the run receipts say so. When the runs are redone, the selector
cost must be unified and the manuscript's cost section reconciled with whatever
is chosen. Verified against source on 2026-09-06; every line reference below was
read, not inferred.

SOURCE OF EACH CLAIM
  Third coordinate is the one-qubit COUNT, not total circuit depth:
    pipelines/scaffold/hh_continuation_scoring.py, _signed_compiled_marginal_components
    fields = {"2q": raw_delta_compiled_count_2q,
              "d":  raw_delta_compiled_depth_2q,
              "1q": raw_delta_compiled_count_1q}
    and result["theta"] = result["shot"] = 0.0
  Zero centering and the nonzero-|marginal| median scale:
    pipelines/scaffold/hh_continuation_scoring.py,
    _zero_centered_signed_hardware_cost_normalization
      medians = {key: 0.0 for key in _HARDWARE_COST_COMPONENTS}
      scale   = median(|raw|) over the strictly nonzero magnitudes
      component_transform = "(2/pi)*atan(raw_signed_delta/scale)"
      weighted_index_formula = "sum(lambda_a*u_a)/sum(lambda_a)"
      score_factor_formula = "1-0.5*weighted_index", bounds [0.5, 1.5]
  Weights actually applied to HH record ranking:
    pipelines/static_adapt/adapt_pipeline.py default block --
      cost_lambda_2q 0.2, cost_lambda_d 0.2, cost_lambda_1q 0.05,
      cost_lambda_theta 0.05, cost_lambda_shot 0.15
    Sum-lambda = 0.65, but theta and shot are hard-zeroed above, so 0.20 of the
    0.65 divisor rides on two identically-zero numerators. That does not merely
    rescale the ranking: the score is DeltaE*(1 - Cbar/2) and DeltaE varies per
    record, so damping Cbar by 0.65/0.45 reweights energy against cost and can
    reorder candidates.
  The manuscript-faithful alternative already exists and was NOT selected:
    pipelines/static_adapt/ra_adapt/engine.py, the
    compiled_marginal_median_mad_signed_arctan_v1 block, lambda 0.30/0.30/0.25,
    third slot raw_delta_compiled_depth.
  (0.30, 0.30, 0.25) in the HH receipts governs the class-representative argmin
    only, not record ranking. See AUDIT-20260906.md line C49 in the
    ADAPT---Paper-I repository.

THIS IS NOT A BUG
All five sealed HH contracts declare zero_centered_signed_arctan_v1 and the code
obeys them. The runs did what they were configured to do. The defect is that the
manuscript describes one functional and six of its reported regimes used another.

WHAT THE MANUSCRIPT CURRENTLY SAYS THAT THE HH RUNS DO NOT SUPPORT
  Paper_I_overleaf_20260818/Paper_I.tex:1150-1153
    "All RA methods use ... relative cost-term weights
     (lambda_2q, lambda_2qd, lambda_c) = (0.30, 0.30, 0.25)."
    False for the six HH regimes on both the weights and the third coordinate's
    identity: there is no lambda_c coordinate in the HH cost vector at all.
  Paper_I_overleaf_20260818/Paper_I.tex:136-138 (abstract)
    "... lowers two-qubit depth by 20.0%-54.8% and total circuit depth by
     24.4%-43.7% across all six regimes."
    The number is measured and correct. But the HH selector never scored total
    circuit depth, so this must not be written as the selector optimizing D_c.
    It is an unscored quantity that improved. Relatedly, RA-Insert LOSES on D_c
    in W-S and I-S, which is exactly what a cost vector omitting D_c predicts.

WHAT THE RERUN SHOULD USE
Recommendation, not a decision -- the choice of functional is the author's:
  coordinates  dN_2q, dD_2q, dD_c   (score the resource the paper reports)
  centering    zero                 (a marginal of zero means "no added cost";
                                     median centering can reward a record for
                                     being cheaper than its peers while it still
                                     grows the circuit, which does not support an
                                     absolute-reduction claim)
  scale        state it explicitly and use one rule everywhere
  weights      state them explicitly; do not let hard-zeroed slots sit in the
               sum-lambda divisor
This is NEITHER current implementation. If it is adopted, the manuscript's
cost section must be rewritten to the zero-centered form, and the sentence at
Paper_I.tex:1150 corrected.

BEFORE SUBMITTING ANY RERUN
Assert the applied functional on a small real cell and read it back out of the
receipt -- a settings block records the request, not what the solver used. Check
the resolved phase1_lambda_*/phase2_lambda_*, the normalization mode, and the
identity of the third coordinate. Keep the existing results separately; they are
the record of what the current manuscript's numbers came from.

DO NOT "FIX" THE CLASS-REPRESENTATIVE ARGMIN -- IT IS ALREADY FAITHFUL
Added 2026-09-06 after a wrong reading was caught by the author. Two separate
code paths select on cost. Only one of them diverges.

  1. CLASS-REPRESENTATIVE ARGMIN (eq:class_representative in the manuscript).
     FIRES, and matches the paper. The six-regime HH costexp protocols set
       semantic_invariants.insertion_equivalence_policy
         = "termwise_cross_component_commutation_min_cost_realization_v1"
     which activates _default_no_prune_costexp_realization_active and the
     min-cost member materialization in adapt_pipeline.py. Weights from
     chtc/paper_i_ra_adapt_repair_20260727/
       ra_adapt_costexp_depthfix_sixregime_20260820_v2_chtc/protocols/*.json:
       weight_2q 0.30, weight_depth_2q 0.30, weight_depth 0.25,
       weight_1q 0.0, weight_size 0.0
     costexp_signed_penalty_total has five slots, but two carry zero weight, so
     the effective objective is exactly (dN_2q, dD_2q, dD_c) at (0.30,0.30,0.25)
     -- the manuscript's three coordinates at the manuscript's weights, over
     raw_delta_compiled_* from backend_transpile_v1, i.e. real routing, not the
     appendix surrogates. The manuscript is correct here. Change nothing.

     Traps that produced a wrong reading once already:
       - sr_snake_route_profile.py declares
         "..._earliest_representative_v1" for its six profiles. Those are
         different routes. The executed six-regime HH arms come from the chtc
         protocol tree above, which overrides it. Do not conclude the argmin is
         dead from the route-profile file alone.
       - min(members) at adapt_pipeline.py:13341 is the earliest-cut
         enumeration used by the earliest-representative routes. It is not the
         costexp path.
       - COSTEXP_INHERITED_SIGNED_PENALTY_WEIGHTS (1.0, 0.0, 0.1, 0.0, 0.01)
         is the inherited default for the two arms that omit an overlay. The
         depthfix six-regime arms carry an explicit overlay and do not use it.

  2. RECORD-RANKING COST TERM K_t (Sec. "Relative resource-cost factor").
     THIS is the divergence documented at the top of this file: zero centering,
     nonzero-|marginal| median scale, dN_1q as the third coordinate, and
     0.20/0.20/0.05 over a 0.65 divisor. Confined to
     pipelines/scaffold/hh_continuation_scoring.py.

So Paper_I.tex:1150 ("All RA methods use ... (0.30,0.30,0.25)") is true of the
class-representative argmin and false of the Phase-I--III ranking weights it
actually refers to. The rerun changes K_t. It must leave the class
representative alone.
